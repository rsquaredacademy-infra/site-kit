Manual Testing Guide

  1. Testing European Visitor Behavior: In Chrome DevTools, press Ctrl+Shift+P (or Cmd+Shift+P), type Sensors, and choose Show Sensors. Under Location, select London or Berlin. Click Clear Cookie & Reload above. The bottom banner dialog will appear.
  2. Testing Keyboard Accessibility: When the banner appears, focus is set to the dialog. Press Tab to cycle between Decline and Accept. Press Escape to verify it declines consent and closes the dialog.
  3. Testing Non-European Visitor Behavior: In the Sensors panel, choose New York, Tokyo, or Off (default). Clear cookie and reload. Notice that no banner is displayed, and GA4 consent default remains granted.
  4. Testing Opt-Out Link: Click the "Opt out of Analytics" button above. Notice the confirmation toast appears and the cookie is set to rsa_consent=denied.
